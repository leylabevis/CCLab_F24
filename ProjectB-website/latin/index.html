Latin

origin: Latin America

styles: salsa, tango, cha-cha, & samba

Latin dance was a collection of lively and rhythmic dance styles that originated in Latin America. Latin dance was characterized by passionate movements, vibrant footwork, and expressive body language.

These dances were often performed in pairs and are rooted in cultural traditions and music. The dances vary in tempo and style, but they all emphasize connection, rhythm, and improvisation.

Latin dance manifests itself as fluid, star-like particles, their movements synchronized with the rhythms of Latin music. The particles create a vibrant, constantly evolving pattern.
