Hip Hop

origin: US

styles: breaking, popping, & locking

Hip hop was a vibrant and energetic dance style that emerged in the 1970s in the US. It’s characterized by its improvisational nature, involving quick footwork, body isolations, and powerful movements.

Dancers express themselves through intricate routines that blend athleticism with creative expression, often influenced by the rhythm and beats of hip hop music. Over time, it evolved into various styles.

As generative art, hip hop appears as a dynamic visual of particles moving in sync with the music, their paths shifting in time with the rhythm, creating a digital dance floor where energy pulses through vibrant bursts of color and motion.
