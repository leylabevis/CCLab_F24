Latin

Origin: Latin America

Styles: Salsa, Tango, Cha-cha, & Samba

About: Latin dance was a collection of lively and rhythmic dance styles that originated in Latin America. These dances were often performed in pairs and were rooted in cultural traditions and music.

Movement Quality: Latin dance was characterized by passionate movements, vibrant footwork, and expressive body language. The dances varied in tempo and style, but they all emphasized connection, rhythm, and improvisation.

Generative Art: Latin dance manifests itself as a red rose, symbolizing passion. The particles create a vibrant, constantly evolving pattern, synchonized to the rhythms of latin music.
